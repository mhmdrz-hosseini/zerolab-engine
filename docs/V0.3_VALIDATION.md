# V0.3 Validation — reference-driven open mold

Date: 2026-09-25. Contract: `docs/MOLD-REFERENCE-DESIGN.md`; plan:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-25-open-mold-reference-fix.md`. This report
supersedes the equivalence claims of `V0.2_REARCHITECTURE.md` (historical).

## What changed in V0.3

1. **Shape-following envelope (primary mode).** The cavity is built from
   per-height kernel slices of the master, offset ⊕(gap + 0.1), unioned over a
   ±gap pull-clearance window and a 29° upward printability ratchet, extended
   vertically through the freeboard. The jacket hugs the master's real
   silhouette (wool, ears, limbs) like the commercial reference instead of the
   V0.2 convex support hull. Rings are resampled radially from a fixed origin
   (the master's bbox center) so the loft's vertex correspondence cannot slide
   and fold the surface.
2. **No coplanar re-cuts.** The seating rim is a 2D ring (plate outline minus
   the widest cavity band profile ⊕0.05); the rail's inner boundary is the
   cavity's mid-section ⊕0.2 unioned over its ±2.5 mm span, verified by an
   intersection guard. The old `(jacket+rail) − cavity` re-cut imprinted
   coincident faces as slivers (41-component regression, fixed).
3. **Rigid-release semantics.** The extraction sim's collision target is the
   MASTER, not the silicone: the cured glove is flexible and conforms, so the
   only rigid obstructions are the master and the base. The sim fails on
   PRESSING-IN (overlap ≥ 0.5 mm³ and non-decreasing) — thin decaying
   construction sheets don't block the ladder; real jams do.
4. **Frame stability.** Base-axis choice is triangle-area-weighted
   (tessellation-stable); explicit `--vertical`/`--split` axes; hard intake
   validation (non-finite, degenerate); explicit `--size` confirmation
   replaces silent rescaling in the CLI.
5. **Dual-BVH extraction sim.** Both geometries carry `boundsTree` — the
   140× slowdown (172 s → 1.2 s on the Z-pull fixture) is gone.
6. **CLI parity.** `scripts/generate_mold.ts` runs the same engine, gates and
   package builder as the worker; hard gate failures block the package and
   exit non-zero. GLB/OBJ intake welds; STL parses raw (the kernel welds the
   facet soup at its own tolerance — pre-welding at 1 µm drops degenerate
   triangles that cracked sculpted surfaces use as patches).

## Known limitations

- The jacket wall's horizontal multiplier (1.45) is provably sufficient for
  the current ratchet bounds; it makes jackets ~45 % heavier than the
  reference's hand-sculpted 4.8 mm walls.
- Top-face wedges: where a master part ends and the cavity narrows to the
  remaining body, the silicone wedge thins below the nominal gap (allowed to
  gap−1.5, documented in the gates). The commercial reference shows the same
  behavior (their p10 dips to 4.0 mm at gap 6–8).
- The 2-piece rigid jacket remains limited: shapes whose fold sheets would
  jam the master on every axis fail honestly and need the multi-panel mode
  (P7, not yet implemented).
- The exported master_base can carry the master's own surface cracks (trimesh
  counts hundreds of components on cracked sculpted STLs; the kernel solid is
  valid and slicers tolerate it).
